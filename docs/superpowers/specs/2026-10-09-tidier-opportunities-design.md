# Opportunities: tidier page — Design

Date: 2026-10-09 (Jack). Same pass as Profitability, Settings, Timesheets, Overview and Jobs the same day.

## Why
Jack uses every part of the page (Pipeline board, Gaps and its Map, Prospects, VoIP Unlimited, Products)
and wants the List view kept, so this is a light tidy: five figure tiles sat above every tab, the page
opened on Gaps rather than the board, and the title read "Sales Opportunities".

## Design
- Header "Opportunities".
- Opens on **Pipeline** (the board). Tabs: Pipeline · Gaps · Prospects · VoIP Unlimited · Products.
- Three figures on every tab: Recurring revenue, Open pipeline, Won this month.
- **Dealer commission** moves to the top of VoIP Unlimited as one line, beside the renewals note.
- Gaps opens on the **Map** unless Clients was the last choice in this browser.

## Removed
Won to date and the Dealer commission tile.

## Unchanged
Board/List, gap rules and checks, emails, prospects, dealer services, products, every saved field.
`show(tab)` takes the same keys.

## Testing
Full suite. Browser preview with sample data: light, dark, 390px.
